Chart Explanations

Portfolio Value Over Time — Historical balance plus projected growth through accumulation and retirement phases.

Contributions vs. Returns — Stacked bar showing how much wealth came from savings versus market gains.

Retirement Runway — Portfolio balance from retirement to end-of-life. Green fill = surplus above safe withdrawal level; red fill = risk zone. Use sensitivity toggles to test bear/bull scenarios.

Monte Carlo — 500 randomized simulations using your return assumption ± volatility. The success rate is how many scenarios ended with $0+ at life expectancy. ≥80% is generally considered healthy.

Retirement Income Sources — Year-by-year retirement income: portfolio withdrawals, CPP, OAS, and extra income.

Annual Withdrawal Rate — Your projected withdrawal rate vs. the 4% safe withdrawal rule benchmark.

Net Worth Over Time — Total assets (portfolio + real estate/other) minus liabilities over time.
